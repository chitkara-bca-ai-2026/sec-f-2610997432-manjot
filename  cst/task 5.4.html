Famous Places in India

Home | Destinations | Foods | Places | Gallery | Contact

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

An iconic ivory-white marble mausoleum on the south bank of the Yamuna River.

2. Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

A honeycomb-shaped pink sandstone palace built for royal women to observe street festivals.

3. Golden Temple, Amritsar

The holiest gurdwara of Sikhism, famous for its gilded architecture and community kitchen.

4. Kerala Backwaters

A serene network of interconnected canals, rivers, and lakes lined with palm trees.

5. Gateway of India, Mumbai

A historic arch monument constructed overlooking the Arabian Sea in Mumbai.
