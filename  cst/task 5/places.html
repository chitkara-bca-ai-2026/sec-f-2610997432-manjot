Famous Places to Visit

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

An ivory-white marble mausoleum on the south bank of the Yamuna river in Agra, commissioned in 1631 by Emperor Shah Jahan.

2. Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

Known as the "Palace of Winds", constructed from red and pink sandstone with 953 intricate windows.

3. Gateway of India, Mumbai

An iconic arch-monument built during the 20th century in Mumbai overlooking the Arabian Sea.

4. Qutub Minar, New Delhi

A 73-meter-high victory tower built in the late 12th century, showcasing early Indo-Islamic architecture.

5. Golden Temple, Amritsar

The holiest Gurdwara and spiritual center of Sikhism, famous for its striking gold-plated exterior.
