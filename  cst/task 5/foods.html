Dish Descriptions

Masala Dosa
A crispy fermented crepe made from rice batter and black lentils, stuffed with spiced mashed potatoes.
Hyderabadi Biryani
A fragrant, slow-cooked rice dish layered with marinated meat, aromatic spices, and saffron.
Gulab Jamun
Deep-fried milk solid balls soaked in a sweet, rose-scented sugar syrup.
