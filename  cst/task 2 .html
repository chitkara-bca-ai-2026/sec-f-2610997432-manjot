College Terminology

Semester
A half-year term in a school or college, typically lasting 15 to 18 weeks.
Syllabus
An outline of the subjects in a course of study or teaching program.
Credits
Units measured to evaluate a student's completion of course requirements.
Hackathon
An event where students collaborate intensively on software projects.
CGPA
Cumulative Grade Point Average used to measure overall academic performance.
